The Secret Gardens of Newnham

Newnham College is renowned for having some of the most beautiful gardens in Cambridge — often called the 'Secret Gardens'. Spread across the college grounds, they offer a rich tapestry of formal plantings, mature specimen trees, wildflower meadows, and carefully curated borders.

This interactive database catalogues the living collections across seven distinct garden areas. It is designed to help visitors, students, and gardening staff identify and locate the remarkable diversity of plant life within Newnham's grounds.

Each garden area can be explored through our hand-drawn maps, with clickable zones revealing the plants found within. The Plant Directory provides a searchable, comprehensive listing of all recorded species.
